# Welcome to Fox Ridge HOA

Welcome to the official website of the Fox Ridge Homeowners Association! We're excited to launch this new platform to keep our community informed and connected.

## What You'll Find Here

This website serves as your central hub for:

- **Community Updates** - Stay informed about board meetings, events, and important announcements
- **Board Information** - Learn about your board members and how to contact them
- **Important Documents** - Access bylaws, rules, and community guidelines
- **Contact Information** - Reach out with questions or concerns

## Getting Started

We encourage all residents to:

1. Bookmark this site for easy access
2. Check back regularly for updates
3. Reach out to board members with any questions

Thank you for being part of the Fox Ridge community!
