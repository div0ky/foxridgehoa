# January Board Meeting Summary

The Fox Ridge HOA Board of Directors held its monthly meeting on January 15, 2025. Here are the key highlights:

## Budget Review

The board reviewed the annual budget and approved several maintenance projects for the upcoming year, including:

- Pool area improvements
- Landscaping updates
- Community entrance enhancements

## Upcoming Events

Mark your calendars for these upcoming community events:

- **Spring Cleanup Day** - March 15, 2025
- **Annual HOA Meeting** - April 10, 2025
- **Community Picnic** - June 20, 2025

## Questions?

If you have questions about any of these items, please contact the board through the contact form on this website.
