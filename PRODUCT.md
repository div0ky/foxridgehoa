# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Residents and homeowners** in Fox Ridge (Wentzville, MO) who want quick access to HOA news, updates, and ways to reach the board from phones and laptops during everyday life at home or on the go.
- **Neighbors and newcomers** who need plain-language context about the association without digging through cluttered or overly formal material.
- **Board members and operators** who use admin tools (`/admin/*`) to publish updates, manage meetings and documents, and keep the site accurate; those surfaces should stay efficient and restrained so they do not fight the calm public tone.

## Product Purpose

Give the Fox Ridge HOA a dependable, readable web presence that centralizes community updates and practical information, lowers friction for contacting leadership, and supports trusted publishing workflows behind authentication. Success means residents complete their tasks (learn what changed, find how to engage) with confidence and minimal confusion.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, and trustworthy. The voice stays neighbor-appropriate and free of hype. Information is prioritized over spectacle; reassurance comes from predictable structure and honest labeling, not from marketing gestures.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing patterns: exaggerated hero metrics, faux-urgency, shallow feature grids, or anything that treats a neighborhood association like a venture-backed growth product.

## Design Principles

- **Clarity beats cleverness.** If a resident hesitates, simplify copy, hierarchy, or navigation before adding ornament.
- **Scan-first content.** Busy people should grasp what matters in seconds; support skim reading with consistent headings and honest summaries.
- **Trust through consistency.** Reuse recognizable patterns across pages so the site feels maintained and intentional.
- **Public calm, focused tools.** Marketing-style energy stays low; authenticated admin views may be denser but should remain orderly and purposeful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** for public pages. Respect system preferences including reduced motion where technically feasible; ensure text and interactive targets remain comfortable for a wide age range typical of HOA communities.
